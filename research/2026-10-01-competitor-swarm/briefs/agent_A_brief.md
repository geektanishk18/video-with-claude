# Agent A: AI-native unified inboxes (pending, never completed)

Read `COMMON_RULES.md` first.

**Context.** FiguredOutAI (India) is building a Unified AI Inbox: one inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and comments, website forms and chat, email and calls. AI reads each conversation, extracts intent, budget, location and timeline, qualifies the lead against the business's own criteria, gives a match score, sorts by priority and routes to the right salesperson, with a CRM behind it. The founder's claim: "an omnichannel AI inbox with proper CRM isn't anyone's product yet." Test the claim; don't agree with it by default.

**Segment.** AI-native unified-inbox startups.
- **Kinso AI (kinso.ai), in depth.** Find its positioning, channels, AI triage and prioritisation, CRM or lead features, pricing and waitlist status, funding, and its Instagram and LinkedIn presence. These pages are likely login-gated: ask the user to log in.
- **Then the 3–5 closest AI-native "one inbox for all messaging channels with AI triage" products launched 2024–2026.** Examples: Beeper-style AI inboxes, Shortwave, Fyxer, Superhuman, Tidio Lyro, or any "AI unified inbox for sales/leads" startup. Prefer the ones closest to business lead qualification.

**Fields per competitor:**
- name, url, positioning (their words), target customer, market (India presence?)
- channels: WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Instagram comments, Messenger, email, website chat/forms, SMS, voice, LinkedIn, Slack, Telegram
- unified inbox, AI field extraction, qualification against custom criteria, scoring/match score, built-in CRM, routing
- pricing and currency, funding/traction, IG/LinkedIn signals
- closeness (0–5) and the gap

Budget: about 30 tool calls, plus the user's logins.
