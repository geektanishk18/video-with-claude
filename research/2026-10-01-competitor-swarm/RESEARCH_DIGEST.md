### Patterns that work

- Qualify by message (2-3 button questions), then send booking link; close on the call not in chat (https://setsmart.io/blog/instagram-sales-funnel)
- Keep qualifying questions minimal; ask service needed and company/role (https://manychat.com/blog/book-more-demos-instagram/)
- Show the scheduler immediately after qualification; multi-form steps and slow calendars reduce bookings (https://www.chilipiper.com/post/form-conversion-rate-benchmark-report)
- Offer near-term slots (same/next day) and send day-before + day-of reminders (https://www.chilipiper.com/post/will-aitken-guide-no-shows)
- Founder's assumption (PDF won't work after a product video): NO direct evidence found either way. Inferred: for a high-ticket custom service, a generic PDF adds a step; a small, specific asset (e.g. 'list of 5 automations for your business type' or a 60s WhatsApp demo) can serve as the value-first … (https://setsmart.io/blog/instagram-dm-scripts)
- Do not follow-gate a B2B audit; policy lists follow-gating as spam (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)
