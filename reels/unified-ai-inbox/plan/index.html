Cursor physics
  • Paths are gentle arcs (quadratic Bézier, control point offset 12% of the distance), never straight lines
  • Move duration = 180 ms + 0.35 ms per pixel, eased in-out cubic; 3-frame settle before any click
  • Hover: target brightens 8% two frames before the click. Click: cursor scales to 0.9 for 3 frames, 36 px ring expands and fades over 8 frames
  • Typing: 40 characters per second with ±15% jitter; caret blinks at 530 ms
  • The cursor never moves while a subtitle emphasis word is popping, so the eye has one target at a time
