07

UI interaction storyboard

Every UI change has a visible cause: a cursor action, a spoken word, or the output of the step before. Word cues are the spoken word the action lands on.

{{UI}}
Cursor physics
  • Paths are gentle arcs (quadratic Bézier, control point offset 12% of the distance), never straight lines
  • Move duration = 180 ms + 0.35 ms per pixel, eased in-out cubic; 3-frame settle before any click
  • Hover: target brightens 8% two frames before the click. Click: cursor scales to 0.9 for 3 frames, 36 px ring expands and fades over 8 frames
  • Typing: 40 characters per second with ±15% jitter; caret blinks at 530 ms
  • The cursor never moves while a subtitle emphasis word is popping, so the eye has one target at a time
08

Animation timing specification

Global
  • 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, {{FRAMES}} frames ({{TOTAL}} s)
  • All cues are computed from timeline.json and subtitles.json, so re-recording a line re-times the video automatically
  • Cuts land 2 frames before the first word of a line (the eye leads the ear)
  • Emphasis animations start on the stressed word's first frame
Motion curves
  • Word-pop: spring (mass 0.6, stiffness 220, damping 14): scale 0.4 → 1.12 → 1 in about 7 frames
  • Subtitle exit: 5-frame fade + scale to 0.96
  • HYPER zoom: 1.0 → 2.6× in 6 frames, ease-in expo, 4-frame motion blur
  • SNAP push: 1.0 → 1.25× in 4 frames, 2-frame white flash at 12%
  • Slow push: 1.00 → 1.06× linear across the shot
  • Whip: 12 frames, 60 px directional blur at the midpoint
  • UI panels: ease-out cubic 12 frames; list reorder spring (stiffness 180, damping 18)
Stillness rules
  • During the 0.80 s turn and 0.90 s stare, the only thing moving is Peter's head
  • From "That's the problem." to "between them." nothing animates except the caption and one slow push
  • Score holds 92% with no motion for the full 0.68 s after "score."
  • After each high-density shot, the next shot starts with at least 8 frames of hold
Sound design cues
  • DING at 0.00; ding cluster from "another" (1.40) and on "everywhere." (4.97)
  • Music bed 112 BPM from 0.50, ducks under Stewie, out on "…Yes." (13.55)
  • Room tone only 13.55 → 21.48; DROP on "handle" (21.48), bed returns full
  • Confirm chord on "score." (30.72) and on "best fit." (47.10)
  • Sip on "nice." (55.38); final chord at 56.59; click to black at 61.18
  • Voice at −16 LUFS, music −28 LUFS under speech, SFX peaks −10 dBFS
