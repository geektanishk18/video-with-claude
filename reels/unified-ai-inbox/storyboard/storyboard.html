What happens when the voiceover arrives

  1. Timing. I transcribe your takes with word timestamps, then every caption and cut on this board snaps to the spoken word. Frames stretch or shrink; the order stays.
  2. Runtime. The board runs 64 s. Frames 44–47 (industries) and 51–54 (payoff) are the easiest trims to reach 60 s.
  3. Build. Remotion needs one npm install, which you asked to approve first.
